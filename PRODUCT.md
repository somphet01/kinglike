# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Home shoppers in Laos choosing mattresses and sleep products for everyday comfort. They compare sizes, firmness, discounts, delivery, and warranty before contacting the store through chat.

## Product Purpose

Kinglike is a premium mattress storefront that helps customers understand the product range, compare prices in Lao kip, save favorites, add items to cart, and start an order conversation with the shop.

## Brand Personality

Premium, calm, assured.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic marketplace clutter, hard-to-read gray text, gimmicky luxury effects, oversized rounded cards, weak price hierarchy, and decorative styling that makes discounts feel less trustworthy.

## Design Principles

Make the price easy to scan first.
Keep the black premium stage and use bright accents only where they help decisions.
Show practical buying confidence: size, savings, delivery, warranty, and chat ordering.
Use product imagery and clear hierarchy instead of generic decoration.
Keep Lao copy readable on mobile and desktop.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, visible focus states, touch targets of at least 44px, readable Lao typography, and reduced-motion-safe interactions.
